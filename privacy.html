Privacy and community rules

Last updated November 14, 2026. Written in plain English on purpose.

The short version

  • Reading the Symposium and the Agora needs no account.
  • If you sign in, we store your email (never shown) and a nickname (shown on what you publish).
  • Your AI key never leaves your browser except to go to the AI company you picked. We never see it.
  • You can delete your account anytime, from the account button.

What the conversations are

The philosophers' lines are written by an AI, imitating how each thinker argued. They are not real quotes, and they can be wrong. Treat them as a way into the ideas, not as the last word on what anyone believed.

What we store, and why

  • Your email address: to identify your account and send sign-in links. It's never shown to anyone.
  • If you sign in with Google or Facebook: they share your name, email and profile picture with our sign-in service (Supabase), which keeps them with your account record. We never show them; only your nickname is public. We don't post anything to Google or Facebook, and we can't see your password.
  • Your nickname: shown on conversations, votes and comments you publish.
  • What you publish: the question, the philosophers' lines, any lines you added at the table, and the summary cards. Published conversations are public.
  • Votes and comments: vote totals are public; who voted what is not. Comments show your nickname.
  • Reports you file, so the site owner can review them.

Conversations you don't publish stay in your own browser only.

Usage stats

The site counts things like visits and which buttons get used, through a cookie-free service called Umami. It never records your questions, the conversations, your email or your AI key.

Who can see what

Everything you publish can be read by anyone. Your email and AI key cannot. The site owner can hide content that breaks the rules below.

Deleting your account

Open the account button, choose Delete account, and type DELETE. Your account, votes and comments are removed right away. Conversations you published stay up as "by a former member"; delete them first, from each conversation's page, if you want them gone too.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove the Symposium from your Facebook settings (Settings → Apps and websites). Then delete your account here as described above, or ask through the contact below and it will be deleted for you.

Age

You must be 13 or older to create an account.

Community rules

  • Argue with ideas, not people. Disagree with Kant as hard as you like. Don't attack other members.
  • No harassment, hate, threats, or sexual content.
  • No spam or advertising.
  • Don't post other people's private information.
  • Don't pass off AI lines as real quotations. If you share something from here, say where it came from.

Anything reported by three different members is hidden until it's reviewed. Breaking the rules can get content removed and accounts banned from posting.

Questions

This is a small personal project. If you have a question about your data, use the contact on the site's GitHub page: github.com/aglenn19/open-symposium.
