How the table plays each philosopher

the exact notes the AI is given, out in the open so you can check them

Every conversation at the table is written by an AI, not by the philosophers. Before it writes a line, it reads the notes below for each person seated. If a note gets someone wrong, or a line in the Agora does, tell us: every correction is read by a person, and accepted ones are listed here with the name of whoever sent them.
